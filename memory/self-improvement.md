# Self-Improvement

## Activation
- The **self-improvement** skill is installed in the workspace.
- It captures learnings, errors, and corrections to `.learnings/` for continuous improvement.
- Important learnings get promoted to `SOUL.md`, `AGENTS.md`, or `TOOLS.md`.

## Workflow
1. **Log** non-obvious fixes, corrections, or better approaches to `.learnings/`.
2. **Review** periodically — especially before big tasks.
3. **Promote** broadly useful learnings into workspace context.

## Key Paths
- Skills: `workspace/skills/self-improving-agent/`
- Logs: `workspace/.learnings/`
