# Cover Prompt Template

Fill in the four placeholders: `[TITLE]`, `[AUTHOR]`, `[SILHOUETTE]`, `[COLOR]`.

```
Create a bold, flat, modern book cover design featuring the title "[TITLE]" and author "[AUTHOR]". The composition shows a large, solid black silhouette of "[SILHOUETTE]" centered on a "[COLOR]" background. The title text completely fills the silhouette shape, warped organically to match its contours. The typography should look hand-drawn, stylized, not ordinary, and full of personality, with some letters stretched, tilted, or oversized for dynamic composition. Use white for title words. The author's name should appear tiny but integrated inside the silhouette, positioned dynamically, using a small, quirky stylized font that still feels part of the composition. Keep the overall look flat, vector-based, and minimalist, with no texture, gradients, or shading. Just clean color blocks and strong contrast. The style should feel quirky, graphic, and bold, like a collectible contemporary art print. Portrait aspect ratio, full book cover composition, no frame or spine.
```
